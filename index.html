About Style Homes

Our Mission

At Style Homes, our first priority is you — the client. Our mission is to listen, adapt, and deliver exactly what makes you feel comfortable, inspired, and proud of your home. Every project starts with your vision, and we make it our goal to bring that vision to life with care and precision.

We specialize in decorative wall panels, wood slat cladding, and luxury Venetian plaster finishes. With more than 13 years of experience in the construction and remodeling industry, our team also provides high-quality renovation services for kitchens, bathrooms, living rooms, and custom interiors. From modern accent walls to complete remodels, we deliver results that are stylish, functional, and built to last.

What sets us apart is our ability to refresh a room in just 1–2 days with budget-friendly solutions that look premium. And beyond design, our work also increases the value of your home. Even small upgrades can make a big difference — boosting market appeal and helping your property stand out with a higher return on investment.

Many homeowners who plan to sell their property choose to work with us before listing. With minimal investments, we help raise the home's resale value significantly. Even if a realtor has already given you an estimate, the right updates can turn a standard sale into a much more profitable one.

Our mission is simple: to create cozy, modern homes where every client feels comfortable, inspired, and truly at home.

Full Interior Work in a Newly Built Home

Complete Interior and Exterior Work for a Newly Built Home

Our team completed a full scope of work for this new home — both interior and exterior finishes, except for painting and last coating. The project features bright white tones, natural wood flooring, and a fresh modern style. It was a pleasure to bring this project to life together with BTN Homes LLC.

Complete Restroom Renovation — Father's House Church

This restroom project was completed for The Father's House Church in collaboration with StyleHome, ProTouch Construction LLC, and several volunteers from the church community. Our team designed and installed modern restroom vanities with quartz countertops, LED mirrors, and matte black fixtures. The light marble walls and dark flooring bring a clean, elegant, and modern look. It was a pleasure to be part of this meaningful project and help refresh an important space for the church.

Modern Geometric Wood Slat Interior Design

This project was completed by StyleHome in collaboration with DreamCabinets and SongLight. Our team installed custom decorative ceiling slats, built from four massive sections, precisely aligned to create a modern geometric look. Additionally, we installed matching wood slats on the walls, maintaining perfect spacing and finish consistency throughout the entire project. The combination of warm wood tones and dark acoustic background gives the space a premium architectural feel.

Crafting Modern Luxury Through Custom Carpentry

Our team completed all custom carpentry work, including doors, windows, staircases, baseboards, and large custom closets and built-in cabinets — every detail designed and installed with precision and elegance. This project represents a perfect blend of modern design and timeless craftsmanship. Precision-Built Modern Deck with Custom Details

This backyard deck project was completed by StyleHome for one of our clients. A project like this takes our team only two days to complete — delivering professional quality and detail in every step.
